Text Encryption / Decryption

Encrypt or decrypt a message with a password

Uses AES-256-GCM with a password-derived key (PBKDF2, 250,000 iterations) — real encryption, done entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Encrypted result

Encryption runs with the Web Crypto API, the same browser-native cryptography used by password managers — nothing here is a custom or toy cipher. That said, security depends entirely on your password: a short or common one is easy to brute-force no matter how strong the encryption algorithm is. Anyone decrypting your message needs the exact same password.

How it works

  1. 01

    Enter your text and password

    Choose encrypt or decrypt mode first.

  2. 02

    A key is derived from your password

    PBKDF2 with 250,000 iterations turns your password into an AES-256 key.

  3. 03

    Copy the result

    Share the encrypted text — only someone with the password can read it back.
